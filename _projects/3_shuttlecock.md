My undergraduate thesis at IIT Kanpur focused on the aerodynamics of rotating shuttlecocks. I worked in Prof. Sanjay Mittal's lab and at the National Wind Tunnel Facility, working with experimental aerodynamics and wind-tunnel data.

### Focus Areas & Tools
- **Research:** Experimental Aerodynamics, Fluid Dynamics, Wind Tunnel Testing
- **Analysis:** MATLAB, Data Processing
